Clean up your messy inbox.

What makes unsubscribely different? Unsubscribely is a free and open-source tool that helps you unsubscribe from unwanted emails and newsletters. It is designed to be simple, fast, and privacy-focused. Unlike other services, unsubscribely does not require you to sign up or provide any personal information. You can simply enter your email address and start cleaning up your inbox right away.

Get Started

Da problem.

Most inboxes are buried in newsletters, promos, or notifications nobody asked for. Unsubscribe links are hidden, sketchy or entirely missing and the free browser extensions that claim to unsubscribe you usually just hide the mess instead of actually removing you from that list!

What Unsubscribely does.

1

See who's spamming your emails

Unsubscribely helps you identify which senders are flooding your inbox with unwanted messages.

2

Read one-click unsubscribe

Fires the actual List-Unsubscribe request not just filtering your emails actaully unsubscribing you from those lists.

3

Auto-archive fallack

No unsubscribe option? Future emails from that sender get filed away automatically instead.

4

Privacy-focused

Unsubscribely does not collect or store any personal information. Your data remains private and secure.

Works well with...

  • Gmail
  • Outlook
  • iCloud Mail
  • Yahoo Mail
  • And possibly more coming soon!

Free. Open Source. No account needed.

Unsubscribely doesn't run on a server that sees your email. Gmail and Outlook connect through their own official sign-in. Your password never touches this app. The full source code is available on GitHub!
